# Nivora Launcher — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 October 2026**

Nivora Launcher is a customizable Android launcher with offline mini-games.

## Information handled by the app

Nivora processes app labels and launcher icons on the device so it can display and launch installed apps. It stores preferences such as favorite apps, recently launched apps, theme selections, font and icon settings, and the user's theme prompt locally in Android app storage.

When a user chooses a wallpaper, Nivora uses Android's system photo picker. The app stores the selected image URI and reads the image only to display it and derive a matching accent color. The selected image is not uploaded by Nivora.

Prism Arcade game state and scores are held locally while a game is running. The games do not need an account or network access.

## Collection and sharing

The current version does not include analytics, advertising, account registration, or an app-controlled network connection. Nivora does not send the locally handled information described above to the developer or to third-party servers.

Android system features, including any device backup or transfer feature enabled by the user or device manufacturer, are governed by the applicable Android and provider settings. A user who wants to keep app preferences exclusively on the device should review the device's backup and transfer settings.

## Permissions and user choice

The app queries activities available to launch so it can provide launcher functionality. The user chooses any wallpaper through Android's system photo picker; Nivora does not request broad media-library access for this purpose.

Users can replace the selected wallpaper or change their settings within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data subject to Android system behavior and any backup settings.

## Children

Nivora is a general-purpose launcher and is not specifically designed for children.

## Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app functionality changes.
